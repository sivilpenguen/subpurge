# Subpurge Privacy Policy

The current policy (English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Français) is published at
**https://sivilpenguen.github.io/subpurge/privacy.html** — source: `docs/privacy.html`.

Short version: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Subscription data stays on the device. The app downloads public price lists (GitHub/jsDelivr) and ECB exchange rates (Frankfurter API); SubPurge Pro purchases are confirmed through RevenueCat with an anonymous ID.
